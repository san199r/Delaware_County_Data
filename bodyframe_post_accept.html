Announcements
Welcome to our online services!

Search Public Records: This provides anyone with an internet connection the ability to search cases and marriages online.

Online Pre-Entry for Marriage License Application: This provides our constituents the ability to submit on-line marriage applications to the Clerk of the Orphans’ Court.

Online Pre-entry of Petition for Probates: This provides attorneys, or those proceeding without an attorney, the ability to complete a pre-entry of Petition for Probate.

If you need further assistance, please do not hesitate to call the office at (610) 891-4400 or email RegOfWills@co.delaware.pa.us.
